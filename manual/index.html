Tang Atari 800 — User manual

An Atari 800/800XL/65XE/130XE recreated on the Sipeed Tang Nano 20K FPGA board, with HDMI video and audio, SD-card disks and cartridges, and a PC link over the board's own USB-C cable. This manual is for owners of the board; it covers setup, everyday use and troubleshooting, not building the design.

Chapters

  1. 1. Overview — what the machine is, what it emulates and what it needs.
  2. 2. Hardware setup — the board, HDMI, SD card, joysticks, keyboard wiring and the status LEDs.
  3. 3. Getting started — ROM files on the SD card, flashing, first power-on and the first disk.
  4. 4. The OSD menu — opening the menu, its items, the file browser and navigating with a joystick.
  5. 5. Options & atari.ini — arrow keys, scanlines, horizontal position, stereo, RAM, modem, and saving settings.
  6. 6. Disks — .atr/.xfd images on D1:–D4:, blank disks, writing, read-only handling and the D4: hard drive.
  7. 7. Cartridges — .car and .rom images, multicarts, Hard Reset versus Soft Reset.
  8. 8. Executables (.xex) — booting binary-load programs directly and ejecting them.
  9. 9. Keyboard — the CH9350 / Pi Pico keyboard link, the Atari-positional key mapping and hotkeys.
  10. 10. Video — the genlocked HDMI output, scanlines and horizontal position.
  11. 11. Audio — HDMI audio and dual-POKEY stereo.
  12. 12. RAM expansion — 128 KB to 1088 KB and when the change applies.
  13. 13. PC Link — atari.py and the desktop app: send, run, type, printer capture, H:, telemetry.
  14. 14. Troubleshooting — no picture, no boot, no sound, keyboard or disk problems, and what the LEDs tell you.
  15. 15. Known issues — behaviour that is understood but not yet fixed.
  16. A. Appendix — cartridge types, devices, file formats, pin reference, glossary, release history, credits.
